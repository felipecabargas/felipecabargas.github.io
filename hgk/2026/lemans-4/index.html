What separated the teams

  • Raw paceThe top two were simply quicker everywhere: both Kart 16 drivers and Kart 6's pair ran medians in the 1:34–1:35s, about two seconds a lap ahead of the midfield. Kart 15 had the fastest driver of the day but its other two were 4–5 s slower.
  • BalanceTeams with two drivers of similar pace kept their lap times steady all race. The closest pairings were — and —; at the other end, some teams had more than two seconds a lap between their drivers.
  • Pit workThe three podium karts lost between 10.5 and 12 s per pit lap, while others in the top ten lost 15 to 20 s. With seven compulsory driver changes, a few seconds per stop is worth a position.
  • TrafficEveryone lost time in a pack, but not equally. The estimated cost of running with 2 or more karts nearby ranged from nothing for Kart 15 to several seconds for Kart 14, whose busy laps were often off-track moments.
